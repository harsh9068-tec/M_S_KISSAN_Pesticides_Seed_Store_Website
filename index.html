DIAGNOSIS DETECTED

Early Shoot Borer (कंसुआ)

Crop: Sugarcane (गन्ना)
Match Confidence 96%
Observed Symptoms:

Dead heart in young shoots, bore holes in cane.

Root Cause:

Chilo infuscatellus larvae boring into shoots.

💊
Recommended Medicine at Store:

Incipio Insecticide (Syngenta)

Dosage & Spray Schedule:

100 ml / Acre in 200 Litres water

Expert Field Advisory:

Spray early in the morning directing nozzle to the base of the cane.
